Privacy

Play and Learn has no accounts, ads, pop-ups or cookies, and it doesn't collect personal information about you or your child.

Counting visits

I use GoatCounter to see how many people visit and which games they play. It doesn't use cookies or store IP addresses. It only keeps totals: the page visited, the site that linked here, and the browser, screen size, language and country.

Saved on your device

The games remember a few small things in your browser, like whether the sound is on and whether a game's first-time animations have already played. If you turn your phone while playing, the game also keeps its current position so it can pick up where it left off. None of this leaves your device, and clearing the site's data in your browser removes it.

Email

If you email me, I only use your message to reply.
