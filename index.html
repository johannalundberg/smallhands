This site started as a project with my son. He's six, just started school, and had gotten curious about math and logic games – so as his mom, and a designer by trade, I wanted to build something with him and for him – a place to play and learn.

It's visually direct and language-free by design. My son has been its most honest tester from day one, and the site keeps growing right alongside him.

I hope you enjoy it too. Feel free to email me if you'd like to say hello or share a thought.
